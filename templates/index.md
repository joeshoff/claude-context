# Communication Templates — Index

Base URL: `https://joeshoff.github.io/claude-context/templates/`

-----

## How to Use This Index

Reference a template by name in any prompt — e.g., “write this using the escalation template” or “use the teams_status format.” I’ll fetch and apply it.

-----

## Templates

### `template_artifact_broadcast.md`

**Use when:** Dropping a report, document, deck, or data artifact into a channel or inbox. No response required. No action needed. The message’s only job is to hand off the artifact.
**Register:** Low-ceremony / informational
**Audience:** Team, peers, leadership

-----

### `template_coaching_note.md`

**Use when:** A specific behavior missed the mark and you’re naming it within 48 hours while context is live. Developmental, not punitive. Leaves the recipient with a decision rule they can apply without you in the room.
**Register:** Direct, developmental
**Audience:** Direct reports (IC or manager)

-----

### `template_corrective_action.md`

**Use when:** Coaching has already happened and this is the formal or near-formal record. Gap is named, evidence is embedded, expectation is a decision rule with a consequence attached.
**Register:** Direct, clinical
**Audience:** Direct report; occasionally CC’d HR or skip-level

-----

### `template_decision_announcement.md`

**Use when:** A decision is made and people need to know what changes and why. No vote pending, no consensus sought. Scales from a Teams message (low-stakes) to email (org-wide or role implications).
**Register:** Direct, low-ceremony
**Audience:** Direct reports, peer leaders, skip-level stakeholders

-----

### `template_escalation.md`

**Use when:** Diagnosis is complete and you need a decision, resource, or blocker removed — not a discussion. The problem has stalled at your level, or inaction now exceeds the cost of the conversation.
**Register:** Direct-urgent
**Audience:** Peer-level leaders, skip-level managers, cross-functional decision-makers

-----

### `template_exec_one_pager.md`

**Use when:** Making a single argument to a VP+ audience that requires a decision, resource, or named response. Too complex for a status update, doesn't warrant a full deck or memo. Covers headcount asks, stalled decisions, org or process changes needing buy-in, and risk escalations. One argument per document.
**Register:** Direct, compressed, owns the judgment
**Audience:** VP and above, skip-level stakeholders, cross-org decision-makers

-----

### `template_external_coordination.md`

**Use when:** Coordinating with a vendor, partner, or cross-org peer on a specific ask, blocker, or next step. Initial outreach, follow-up, or clarification. Not for formal escalations or contract negotiations.
**Register:** Professional-direct
**Audience:** Vendors, partners, peer orgs at WGU

-----

### `template_management_inquiry.md`

**Use when:** You have a data point, discrepancy, or observed gap and need a direct report to account for it and name their plan. The diagnosis is already formed — the question carries it. Accountability without triggering defensiveness.
**Register:** Direct, low-ceremony
**Audience:** Direct reports (engineering managers, tech leads)

-----

### `template_status_update_lateral.md`

**Use when:** Weekly or periodic status artifact distributed to peers and cross-functional partners. This is a document, not a message — the message that distributes it is an `artifact_broadcast`. Originally SEM 1:1 prep; now shared broadly because peers found the signal useful. Public version strips personnel details; private version includes suggested questions and coaching context.
**Register:** Direct, diagnostic
**Audience:** Peers, cross-functional partners, adjacent leadership

-----

### `template_status_update_up.md`

**Use when:** Weekly or ad-hoc status update sent upward. Recipient needs current state, risk posture, and any blocked decision — without extracting it from narrative.
**Register:** Direct, compressed, owns the judgment
**Audience:** VP and above

-----

### `template_teams_inquiry.md`

**Use when:** Asking a specific question or requesting a decision over Teams. A meeting isn’t warranted and email would be over-formatted. One question (two if they share context). Never for building consensus.
**Register:** Direct, compressed, peer-to-collegial
**Audience:** Direct reports, peers, cross-functional partners

-----

### `template_teams_status.md`

**Use when:** Recurring or ad-hoc status drops in a Teams channel. Verdict at a glance (with emoji signal when channel culture supports it), then evidence and mechanism. Checks Jira surface against RAID.
**Register:** Internal
**Audience:** Engineering leadership, SEMs, direct reports
